SELF-DIAGNOSTIC ADDITIONS TO THE V4 ANALYSIS BRIEF
====================================================

These additions are prepended/inserted into the standard v4 Analysis Brief
for self-diagnostic analysis of the author's own fiction.


--- INSERT BEFORE "READING MANDATE" ---

SELF-DIAGNOSTIC CONTEXT

This is the author's own fiction being analyzed for instinctive pattern
discovery. The question is not "what does this story use?" but "what does
this author instinctively do?" — what technique preferences, recurring
patterns, and distinctive choices appear across the writing?

Emphasize: recurring technique preferences (does the author default to DT or
FID? what interiority techniques appear most?), distinctive choices (what does
this author do that is unusual or characteristic?), and instinctive patterns
(what shows up without apparent deliberate design?). These patterns ARE the
primary finding.

This analysis feeds into a cross-story synthesis comparing 7 texts spanning
2015-2025 across three fandoms (Pokemon, MLP:FiM, Equestria at War). Each
per-story analysis is independent — the subagent reads only one text. The
synthesis session reads all 7 analysis outputs. The analysis should be self-
contained: report what this text shows without reference to other texts.


--- INSERT INTO "READING MANDATE" AFTER THE ITALIC-MARKERS PARAGRAPH ---

UNFINISHED AND PARTIAL TEXT

If this text is an unfinished work (the story is ongoing or was abandoned
before completion), analyze what exists. Note the incompleteness as context
rather than a limitation — "the story's Theme Propositions are incomplete
because the story ends mid-arc" is a finding, not an apology. Run the full
analysis structure including all sections; mark sections that are genuinely
inapplicable to an incomplete text as N/A with a brief note on why.

If this text is partial text (a fragment or draft, not a story with a
beginning), treat it as prose-technique evidence rather than a story to be
analyzed for arc and theme. The full analysis structure still applies — the
Perspective Analysis, Inference Analysis, and Framework Fit sections are
productive even on partial text. Story Map, Theme Propositions, and Bond
Analysis may be N/A.


--- APPEND TO "VOCABULARY REFERENCE" AFTER THE DT-BASED KNOWLEDGE ASYMMETRY PARAGRAPH ---

Additional perception-gap delivery mechanisms (from the 112-story corpus
analysis). These are recognition vocabulary — name them when they appear:

First-person unreliability: The narrator is a character, and the reader can
distrust the narration itself. When a first-person narrator says "I wasn't
bothered," the reader may infer from context that the narrator was deeply
bothered. M4-adjacent through narrator unreliability rather than FID.

Dual-POV structural dramatic irony: Reader holds knowledge from one
focalizer's chapters while reading the other's, producing sustained
asymmetric awareness. Neither Latent nor Perception Gap nor Development in
the classical sense.

Strategic opacity: The narration withholds interiority at a moment when the
reader expects access, producing inference from the withholding itself.

Narrated denial: The narrator states a character's self-assessment that the
accumulated narrative evidence contradicts. The gap between the narration's
claim and what the reader has observed does the work.

The adversarial inner voice: A character's DT operates as a hostile
interlocutor to their own consciousness, producing a split-self effect.

The Mother voice: A character's interiority channels the voice and
judgments of a parental figure, producing a second consciousness within
the character's own narration.

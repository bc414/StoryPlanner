# exploration-of-own-fiction — leads

## Method

Retroactive. This study is the registry's account of WU1.3, executed on 2026-09-01 under
revision 1 of the method, before studies, reading protocols, the runner or question lists
existed. Scale: pathfinder over analyses. Seven self-diagnostic v4 analyses, one per text,
were produced by subagents under the brief additions in
`docs/analysis-briefs/v4-self-diagnostic-additions.txt` and read by one session in one
context, which wrote `docs/v3-framework/WU1.3-own-voice-synthesis.md`, this study's source
and full account; this artifact is its index of leads. The same day, a post-WU review with
Brian source-checked the analyses' FID classifications against the texts by line number
(every text except THLB) and drew a seeded random sample of italic lines (seed 40); the
source's Correction section records that review, and its items are carried below as leads
and corrections. No protocol, no hash, no arms, no runner run; models and harness were not
recorded. Loci are the analyses (`Reading Archive Analyses/`, each `-1m.txt`) and, where
the review read a text, the text by line. One arm: no disagreement was measured.

## Questions in view

None from a question list; the lists did not exist. The reading was done with the
revision-1 card's questions in view: what Brian's instinctive toolkit is, in the corpus
vocabulary; where his FID falls on the narrator-presence spectrum; whether the pre-FiM
Pokemon stories and the post-FiM texts differ in technique; whether the naive chapters'
fabula-through-dialogue is replaced in the later texts; and, from Brian's recall, whether
his bond obstacles differ from the corpus's and whether dual-POV irony is foundational.

## Leads

**Interiority by text, as the analyses profile it.** THLB (2015–17, first person
alternating): DT very heavy, told narration secondary, no FID possible. Wish (2020): DT
heavy, told narration, FID brief and transitional. TEatS (2020–21): DT pervasive, told
narration, FID absent. NTL (2021–23): DT moderate with behavioral proxy, dialogue, FID rare.
GIYC (2024–25): DT heavy with behavioral proxy, FID subordinate and scattered. Falldale (Nov
2025): dialogue primary, behavioral proxy, FID sparse and shallow, DT absent. Naive TLTT
(June 2025): dialogue primary, told narration, DT sparse, FID sparse. M4 absent from all
seven. DT is absent in the two EaW texts, both war or tragedy; the FiM-genre text (GIYC)
keeps heavy DT while adding FID.

**FID passages, source-checked.** Naive TLTT line 93, "frantic, muddy countrymare", holds
as AJ's own word, idiom and bitter repetition inside her realization of how others see
her; "that blasted title" likewise. GIYC line 199, "she was so much larger than life that
she could brush off all the fashion reporters with attitude and pizzazz", is Rarity's
focalized section watching Fluttershy arrive, in Rarity's vocabulary; "Be bold like
Rarity" (about line 1880) is Fluttershy quoting Rarity's line 475. Falldale FID holds at
Rocky's register at lines 149, 153, 241 and 245, with two uncited instances at 241 ("Ivy
Dazzle, no, *Ixodida* flew back") and 245 ("Alone. No farmhoof to help him anymore, no
thoughts, just hay bales"); Ixodida's line 379 is told interiority with a narrator figure.
THLB's classifications were not source-checked.

**Narrator register rendering the character's experience.** GIYC's "pink curtain" is
narration at every occurrence (lines 103, 117, 245, 1316, 1800, 2118, 2142) and never in
Fluttershy's dialogue; line 103 introduces it as a coining in the same sentence as the
literal mane ("tried to hide behind her mane, but her usual pink curtain was tied back"),
line 119 rhymes it with the literal stage curtain she hides behind, line 2142 renders an
involuntary action in a figure she is not consciously deploying; the motif runs across all
five chapters and shifts from involuntary to chosen. "Her big disobedient eyes" (line
2178) is the same shape. GIYC is the one text where the source confirms this; Wish's
asides ("Don't get the wrong idea!", line 25; "now we know where Jason gets his late night
tendencies from", 37; the random student's prayer, 113) address the reader about the world
and are an intrusive narrator, a Wish-only register.

**Told interiority displacing an established register.** NTL line 8649: Lily's register
is set in the preceding clauses ("snuggle", "pitter-patter"), then the narrator names her
state from outside ("an itch that gnawed at her, forcing her to imagine what it was like
to live under pressure from others every day"). NTL line 6683: "Windie felt like a Stone
Edge was slicing through his chest" is a told sensory simile, followed by the guilt stated
outright in marked DT ("Is Luneland worth preserving?"); the line is Windie's, and the
analysis mis-transcribed it as Chadwyck's. Brian's adjudication, 2026-09-01: both are told
interiority, neither FID nor blend.

**The italic-line sample.** Of the 621 italic spans of four or more words across the seven
texts, a seeded sample of forty (seed 40); thirteen excluded as flashback dialogue or
untagged telepathy; twenty-seven marked thoughts. Six stand against contradicting evidence
the reader holds: THLB 3717 (denial against Maple's chapters), GIYC 1714 (rationalised
attraction), TEatS 5691 ("just being here for my best friend" after the kiss), THLB 3597
(disbelief at Squash the reader was set up to precede), and NTL 2693 and THLB 3811, both
weak. Twenty-one state a feeling or realization the surrounding text has shown or tagged,
in recurring shapes: restatement after the state is shown or tagged (ten; "eyes widened in
shock. *…had the courage to run away?* she thought incredulously"), plan or status
announcement (two), told evaluation (three), told realization at an arc climax (two,
including naive TLTT 362's "*I am a general*" after the behavioral proxy showed it), comic
voice (two), a revelation delivered in DT (one), a Mother voice (one, NTL 4309). Per text:
THLB three of five, TEatS one of seven, NTL one of seven. Telepathy in TEatS and NTL and
flashback italics in THLB inflate raw italic counts by roughly a third. Classification by
Claude; the five borderline items (the revelation, the climax, the comic voice, the two
weak) are unadjudicated.

**Dual-POV structural dramatic irony.** THLB: alternating first person, the reader holding
both characters' feelings while neither knows the other's, which the analysis calls the
story's "most sophisticated technique", operating "throughout the middle third". Wish:
Jason's and Lydia's sections. TEatS: alternating close third plus telepathy the surrounding
characters cannot hear. NTL: villain-POV inserts (Count Ignatio's scheming). GIYC: the
reader knows Fluttershy's crush before any character, and Rarity's jealousy while Fluttershy
does not. Absent in Falldale and naive TLTT, where the reader's structural knowledge of the
EaW setting supplies the gap. Present in THLB, the earliest published text.

**Wrong self-assessments stated in DT.** GIYC "I must be a horrible pony" against four
chapters of contrary evidence; TEatS Valerie's "a burden" against Shane's demonstrated
devotion; Wish Jason's "not ready for a formal relationship" against his behavior; THLB
Hickory's "she seemed happier when she was with me" against Maple's chapters. Hickory's
self-serving readings of Spruce and of Maple's blushes are a first-person narrator's bias.

**The inner debate.** DT as an argument between impulses: THLB ("Yes! Now I can have
Maple!" / "No, don't think like that", in both protagonists), Wish ("I'm totally not being
a creep"), TEatS (Valerie's guilt cycle), GIYC (Fluttershy's self-recrimination as a
hostile interlocutor), NTL (Windie's admiration versus rejection of nobles). Absent from
Falldale and naive TLTT.

**Techniques not found.** Strategic opacity and narrated denial appear in none of the
seven texts.

**Narrator voice and perspective mode.** Every multi-focalizer analysis reports one
narrator register regardless of focalizer: THLB "both narrators use the same register",
TEatS "it does not shift between focalizers", NTL "the voice stays consistent", GIYC "warm,
gently comic, affectionate" for both. Characters are distinguished by dialogue voice and DT
content. TEatS and NTL, labelled omniscient by NotebookLM, are rotated soft-limited third
in the analyses: POV shifts at scene breaks, knowledge boundaries kept, brief villain-POV
inserts as irony devices; the narrator stays close to the focalizer without dissolving into
the consciousness. Every text sits in the same compressed soft-limited range.

**Bond obstacles.** THLB structural (Maple mated to Spruce) with characterological secondary
(Maple's fear and dependency); Wish communicative and psychological; TEatS structural
(royal marriage) with characterological secondary (Valerie's self-blame); NTL structural
twice (duty to different cities; a class barrier); GIYC communicative and characterological,
with Fluttershy's suppression pattern reading as embedded in her rather than chosen;
Falldale structural (the changeling invasion), non-romantic. Communicative obstacles are
primary only in the two shortest texts.

**Behavioral proxy vocabularies.** Pokemon: ears (drooping, perking, flopping), tails
(clenching, wagging, wrapping), flames (brightening, sputtering, dimming), fur (sparking,
puffing), type-specific (blushing through Flareon fur, Leafeon vine extension). Pony:
wings (snapping up, drooping, unfurling), hooves (scuffing, stomping). External action in
the EaW texts: spitting at hooves, refusing food, and the general-title refusal pattern
tracked across both naive chapters from refusal through strategic deployment to internal
acceptance. Proxy sophistication rises gradually from THLB's blushing and warmth through
NTL's physiology system to GIYC.

**Comedy positions.** GIYC: Pinkie's schemes drive the Filli Vanilli plot, the turkey-call
opening sets the warm register before later trauma. NTL: Terry's Substitute pranks and puns
("plant-a-tion!") between siege and economics chapters; Chadwyck's bravado. THLB: Hooves
and Falc as contrast to romantic tension. Wish: the narrator's asides. Falldale: an opening
of giggling foals and an antidote joke that every later page registers against the
invasion. Positions recur: character introductions, after intensity, before a reveal, as
irony setup. Absent from naive TLTT and from Falldale after its tonal turn.

**Counterarguments, source-checked.** TEatS: Seismitoad's systemic-injustice argument,
validated by independent evidence. NTL: the dukes' stability argument, April's "not far
enough", Max's pragmatism, each granted validity. Naive TLTT: Comet Shine's concession about
Celestia. THLB: the analysis credited Cedar with acknowledging the Guard's culpability for
Spine; the source has Cedar denying it twice (line 5117, "Then that's his own fault, not
ours! We didn't kill Spine"; again at 5149) while Hickory's summary at 5143 pivots to the
Guard's grievances; the Guard's position (Spine harassed Hazel, the fight was defensible)
and Splint's (a friend died after a beating) both hold and neither is defeated, delivered
through dialogue with the reader outside the Guard's denial. GIYC: Rarity's ambition is the
pro-visibility position, treated as legitimate through the Hoity Toity plot and held against
Fluttershy's harm, unresolved. Wish has none; Falldale's changeling history stays in
subtext.

**Fabula delivery.** Naive TLTT delivers nearly all worldbuilding through characters
explaining things to AJ; its unfinished Chapter 2 sections degrade into outline bullets of
propositions (the Fleur Bloom lecture). TEatS: Helping Hand and telepathy carry partnership
and intimacy as mechanics. NTL: Kyukonais's prosperity is shown being built (clearing
forest, sawmills, banks; chapters 8–9). GIYC: the modeling trauma arrives through proxy and
DT; the "armscye" speech reads as absorbed trauma. Falldale: Ixodida's geopolitical
testimony is an exposition monologue of about 1,500 words beside action and Rocky's
responses. Planned wrong inferences: THLB's Squash betrayal, TEatS's Firinia-as-saviour;
few others. Sequenced disclosures: TEatS's Elliot identity, THLB's Raider origin, NTL's
progressive feudal exposure, naive TLTT's Celestia revelation layered through several
characters, Falldale's Ivy as Ixodida. The reader generally knows what the characters know.

**Demonstration over declaration.** THLB: twenty chapters of training as bonding,
near-confessions suppressed, nuzzles and paw-holding, the declaration last. TEatS:
physical affection in every chapter. NTL: Terry teaching Lily to read, Windie giving Helena
his bed, Helena warming Windie. GIYC: four chapters of mutual rescue before the crush is
named. Wish: the Wish mechanic as battle tactic and involuntary confession at once.

**Asymmetric access.** THLB: Hickory slightly more narration, his feelings disclosed from
Chapter 1. Wish: Jason about 60% of screen time and the deepest passage (the detention
reverie). TEatS: roughly symmetric, Shane's DT strategic and Valerie's raw. NTL: Helena
dominant, Windie co-protagonist from Chapter 11. GIYC: Fluttershy primary, Rarity's
romantic potential withheld.

**World inference ending in structure.** THLB: Spruce's promise-breaking, Cedar's backstory,
the Custap berry promise. TEatS: class prejudice, the Payr and Nydyhan contrast, Shane's
speech evolution. NTL: the economic-development chapters. GIYC: the modeling scenes.
Theme-terminal chains arrive as culminations of this groundwork.

**Embedded texts and dreams.** THLB: Hickory's romantic dream (Chapter 6). TEatS: Elliot's
letter (Chapter 21), the intelligence that saves the kingdom arriving as a document. None in
the other five texts.

**Design targets.** Information architecture: THLB's dual-POV knowledge states, TEatS's
Elliot reveal, NTL's feudal exposure, naive TLTT's graduated Celestia revelation.
Structural correspondence: THLB's Custap berry (childhood to adult fulfilment), TEatS's
five-fold separation and reunion, NTL's Helena's Drought as political metaphor, GIYC's
confession-per-chapter structure, Falldale's comedy becoming irony. Reader stance
trajectory: no analysis identifies a designed shift between observation, inhabitation,
solidarity and irony across scenes, except THLB's movement from curiosity to frustration to
satisfaction.

**Pre-FiM and post-FiM.** FID negligible in all four Pokemon texts (TEatS Chapter 2
verified; NTL Chapter 1 cited by its analysis, unverified), present and subordinate in GIYC
and naive TLTT, absent in Falldale, the newest text, which dropped DT to zero without
gaining FID. Canon recontextualisation as method (episodes resequenced and deepened) appears
in GIYC alone. Narrative warmth is stable, more restrained in the two war texts.

**Framework fit remarks in the analyses.** Dialogue-as-psychology in the war texts sits
between the reader observing and the text stating. Didactic worldbuilding (NTL's economics,
naive TLTT's geopolitics) is learning alongside the character. Accumulation operates below
the scene level.

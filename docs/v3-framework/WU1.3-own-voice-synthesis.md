# WU1.3: Own-Voice Synthesis

Executed: 2026-09-01
Sources: 7 self-diagnostic v4 analyses of Brian's fiction (2015-2025)

## The Interiority Hierarchy

The single most consistent finding across all 7 texts: Brian's instinctive
interiority toolkit is **DT-dominant, FID-sparse, dialogue-heavy**, with
behavioral proxy as the strongest non-verbal technique. The hierarchy varies
by text but the architecture is stable:

| Text | Primary | Secondary | FID | M4 |
|------|---------|-----------|-----|-----|
| THLB (2015-17) | DT (very heavy) | Told narration | N/A (1st person) | Absent |
| Wish (2020) | DT (heavy) | Told narration | Brief, transitional | Absent |
| TEatS (2020-21) | DT (pervasive) | Told narration | Absent | Absent |
| NTL (2021-23) | DT (moderate) + behavioral proxy | Dialogue | Rare | Absent |
| GIYC (2024-25) | DT (heavy) + behavioral proxy | FID (subordinate) | Present, scattered | Absent |
| Falldale (Nov 2025) | Dialogue | Behavioral proxy | Sparse, shallow | Absent |
| Naive TLTT (June 2025) | Dialogue | Told narration | Present, sparse | Absent |

**M4 (Perception Gap) is absent from every text.** No story achieves the
FID-based inhabitation that creates the reader-inhabits-while-knowing dynamic.
The mechanism ceiling is M3 (Development) with M4-adjacent effects through
DT-based knowledge asymmetry and dual-POV structural dramatic irony.

**FID is not absent from the toolkit — it is not the default.** GIYC shows
the clearest FID capacity: the "pink curtain" motif, Fluttershy's
self-recrimination passages ("frantic muddy countrymare" has an echo in naive
TLTT's FID passage of the same construction), and several passages channeling
Fluttershy's attraction. The naive TLTT has 5 identifiable FID instances
including the most sustained passage in the corpus ("She quickly realized
nopony would take a frantic, muddy countrymare seriously..."). FID appears
when the character's self-assessment or half-conscious feelings are being
channeled — it does not appear as the default narration mode.

**The consequence for the framework:** The DT/FID axis that determines the
mechanism ceiling in the 112-story corpus operates here as predicted. Brian's
fiction caps at M3 because his instinctive interiority technique creates
sympathetic observation (the reader watches the character think via DT) rather
than sympathetic inhabitation (the reader thinks as the character via FID).
This is a stable authorial preference, not a limitation — the fiction achieves
emotional power through other means (accumulation, structural irony, dialogue).

## The DT/FID Ratio by Story

The Pokemon stories (2015-2023) show uniformly heavy DT usage. DT appears on
nearly every page of THLB, TEatS, and Wish. NTL moderates somewhat — DT is
still the primary interiority tool but behavioral proxy has risen to share the
load.

The post-FiM texts split:
- **GIYC** (MLP, 2024-25): Heavy DT plus the most FID of any text. This is
  the story where FID emerges as a subordinate but genuine technique.
- **Falldale** (EaW, Nov 2025): Zero DT, near-zero FID. Dialogue carries all
  interiority. This is the most dramatic departure from the baseline.
- **Naive TLTT** (EaW/MLP, June 2025): Sparse DT (5 instances), sparse FID.
  Dialogue dominates.

The DT disappearance in the two EaW texts correlates with genre (war/tragedy)
more cleanly than with the FiM reading period. Falldale's total DT absence
and naive TLTT's sparse DT may reflect the tonal demands of the material —
a first tragedy and a war narrative pull toward externalized narration. GIYC,
which is MLP romance (the same genre as the analyzed corpus), retains heavy
DT while adding FID. Whether the FiM reading caused the FID emergence in GIYC
or whether the MLP genre context invited it is not separable from this evidence
alone.

## Narrator-Character Blend Spectrum (Hypothesis 030)

Where does Brian's writing fall on the FID spectrum from full
character-voice takeover to narrator-literary-register-visible passages?

**Brian's FID, when it appears, channels character self-assessment and
emotion, not literary observation.** The FID instances across the corpus:
- "Frantic, muddy countrymare" (naive TLTT) — AJ's self-image
- "That blasted title" (naive TLTT) — AJ's attitude
- "The whole outfit felt like a prison by now" (GIYC) — Fluttershy's evaluation
- "Her trusty mane" / "the pink curtain" (GIYC) — Fluttershy's name for her
  own mane's protective function
- "She was simply stunning in that dress, the dress Fluttershy had made!"
  (GIYC) — Fluttershy's admiration

None of these show the narrator's own literary register rendering the
character's perception. They are all the character's own vocabulary and
evaluation channeled through narrator voice. Brian's FID is at the
character-voice end of the spectrum — when the narrator dissolves into the
character, it adopts the character's words, not a literary rendering of the
character's perception.

The "narrator-character blend" as hypothesized (narrator's literary vocabulary
rendering a character's subjective perception) does not appear in Brian's
fiction. His FID is straight character-voice FID, brief and subordinate to DT.
This is evidence that the blend, if it exists as a distinct technique, is not
part of Brian's instinctive toolkit.

## Dual-POV Structural Dramatic Irony

**This is Brian's primary technique for producing reader-character knowledge
asymmetry.** It appears in 5 of 7 texts:

- **THLB**: The alternating first-person creates sustained irony — the reader
  knows both characters' feelings while neither knows the other's. The analysis
  calls it "the story's most sophisticated technique" operating "throughout the
  middle third."
- **Wish**: The reader knows both Jason's and Lydia's feelings from their
  respective sections.
- **TEatS**: The reader holds both Shane's and Valerie's interiority through
  alternating close third + telepathic communication the surrounding characters
  cannot hear.
- **NTL**: Multiple focalizers create knowledge asymmetry between reader and
  individual characters (e.g., the reader knows about Count Ignatio's scheming
  from villain-POV inserts).
- **GIYC**: The reader knows Fluttershy's crush before any character does; the
  reader knows Rarity's jealousy while Fluttershy doesn't.

Only Falldale (primarily single-POV) and naive TLTT (strictly single-POV) lack
this. In both, dramatic irony is created through externalized means — the
reader's structural knowledge of the EaW setting creates the gap.

Brian's recall that dual-POV structural dramatic irony "may be the foundational
mechanism that got him to want to write in the first place" is supported by the
evidence: THLB, his earliest published story, already deploys it as the central
technique. It persists across all multi-focalizer texts regardless of fandom or
period.

## Pre-FiM vs Post-FiM Technique Comparison (Hypothesis 039)

Pre-FiM Pokemon stories (THLB 2015-17, Wish 2020, TEatS 2020-21, NTL 2021-23)
vs post-FiM texts (GIYC 2024-25, Falldale Nov 2025, naive TLTT June 2025):

**What shifted:**
- **FID emergence**: Absent or negligible in all four pre-FiM texts. Present
  (subordinate) in GIYC and naive TLTT. Still absent in Falldale. This is the
  clearest shift but it correlates with MLP/EaW fandom context as much as with
  chronology.
- **Behavioral proxy sophistication**: NTL (2021-23, pre-FiM) already shows
  Pokemon-physiology proxy as a developed technique. GIYC's "pink curtain" motif
  — a single proxy that evolves across five chapters from involuntary to chosen
  — is the most sophisticated proxy work in the corpus. The sophistication
  increase is gradual, not a step change.
- **Canon recontextualization as method**: GIYC uses canon episodes as raw
  material, resequencing and deepening them. This technique is absent from the
  Pokemon stories (which are entirely original worlds). It is specific to MLP
  fiction and may reflect the influence of reading 100+ MLP fanfics that do this.

**What did not shift:**
- **DT dominance**: Still heavy in GIYC (the one MLP text).
- **Narrative voice warmth**: Stable across all texts. Falldale and naive TLTT
  are more restrained, but this correlates with genre (war/tragedy) rather than
  period.
- **Uniform narrator voice across focalizers**: Stable across all multi-POV texts.
- **Dual-POV structural dramatic irony**: Present from THLB onward wherever
  multiple focalizers exist.
- **M4 absence**: Unchanged. No story achieves canonical Perception Gap.

**Assessment:** The pre-FiM to post-FiM shift is real but modest. The clearest
change is FID emergence in GIYC (the MLP story), which could reflect either
the FiM reading influence or the MLP genre context inviting the technique. The
core toolkit — DT-heavy, warm narrator, dialogue-dominant, structural irony
through dual-POV — is stable across the decade. THLB's first-person alternating
POV already shows the perspective-restriction instincts and dual-POV irony that
persist in all later work. The prediction that "perspective awareness appeared
from nothing" was already ruled out; the prediction that "the specific toolkit
shifted" finds partial support in FID emergence and behavioral proxy
sophistication but cannot separate the FiM-reading effect from maturation and
genre effects.

## Bond Obstacle Architecture

Stories with bonds: THLB, Wish, TEatS, NTL (x2), GIYC, Falldale (non-romantic).

| Story | Primary Obstacle | Secondary |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| THLB | Structural (Maple is mated to Spruce) | Characterological (Maple's fear/dependency) |
| Wish | Communicative/psychological | — |
| TEatS | Structural (royal marriage) | Characterological (Valerie's self-blame) |
| NTL (Helena/Windie) | Structural (duty to different cities) | — |
| NTL (Terry/Lily) | Structural (class barrier) | — |
| GIYC | Communicative + characterological | — |
| Falldale | Structural (changeling invasion) | — |

**Structural obstacles dominate.** 5 of 7 bonds have structural primary
obstacles — conditions embedded in the world that would not dissolve if the
characters simply talked. Communicative obstacles (would resolve with one honest
conversation) appear as primary only in the two shortest texts (Wish, GIYC).
Characterological obstacles (one person must transform before the relationship
is possible) appear as secondary in 2 stories.

Brian's recall that his stories would differ from the corpus's
characterological dominance is confirmed. His instinctive obstacle architecture
intertwines bonds with parallel structural/political conflicts rather than
making the bond itself the barrier. Even in GIYC, where the obstacle is
communicative, Fluttershy's suppression pattern is structural to her
character — it's the same pattern that made her endure modeling, and it operates
as a world condition (her anxiety is embedded, not choosable).

## Behavioral Proxy Vocabulary

Brian's fiction develops a rich behavioral proxy vocabulary that adapts to each
fandom's physicality:

**Pokemon-specific proxies (THLB, Wish, TEatS, NTL):**
- Ears: drooping (distress), perking (attention), flopping (embarrassment)
- Tails: clenching (anger), wagging (happiness), wrapping around partner (comfort/intimacy)
- Flames: brightening (confidence/anger), sputtering (distress), dimming (exhaustion)
- Fur: sparking (Shinx electricity = anxiety), puffing (alarm)
- Type-specific: blushing through fur (Flareon heat), vine extension (Leafeon comfort)

**Pony-specific proxies (GIYC):**
- The "pink curtain" (Fluttershy's mane): closing in (threat), draping over cheeks (embarrassment), weaponized for cover, evolving from involuntary to chosen
- Wings: snapping up (surprise), drooping (defeat), unfurling (attraction)
- Hooves: scuffing (reluctance), stomping (emphasis)

**External-action proxies (Falldale, Naive TLTT):**
- Spitting at hooves (contempt)
- Refusing food (rejection of friendship)
- The general-title verbal refusal pattern (naive TLTT, the most sophisticated
  single behavioral proxy in the corpus — tracked across both chapters from
  refusal through strategic deployment to internal acceptance)

The proxy vocabulary is the author's strongest non-DT interiority technique.
GIYC's "pink curtain" motif — a single proxy that evolves in meaning across
five chapters — is more sophisticated than any FID passage in the corpus.
NTL's Pokemon-physiology proxy system is extensive enough to serve as an
emotional language. This is a genuine instinctive strength.

## Comedy Placement

Comedy appears as a structural force in 5 of 7 texts:

- **GIYC**: Comedy and emotional devastation coexist in the same chapters.
  Pinkie's schemes drive the Filli Vanilli plot. Comedic setups precede
  emotional climaxes. The turkey-call opening establishes the warm register
  that makes later trauma hit harder.
- **NTL**: Terry's Substitute pranks and puns ("plant-a-tion!") provide comic
  relief between heavy political/economic content, making 160K words of
  civilization-building palatable. Chadwyck's bravado.
- **THLB**: Hooves (gossip, energy) and Falc (self-aggrandizing persona)
  provide comic contrast to the romantic tension.
- **Wish**: The narrator's comedic personality (fourth-wall-adjacent asides,
  self-aware commentary) establishes the story's accessible tone.
- **Falldale**: Opens with comedy (foals giggling, older mare's antidote joke)
  that becomes devastating dramatic irony — every laugh registers against the
  reader's knowledge that the invasion is real.

**Structural positioning:** Comedy appears at character-introduction beats
(Hooves, Falc, Chadwyck, Pinkie), after intensity (Terry's jokes during
siege chapters), and as dramatic-irony generators (Falldale's opening,
Wish's narrator asides about Jason's denial). This confirms comedy-as-moment
placement as something Brian instinctively designs.

Absent from naive TLTT (the material is too compressed and partially
unfinished) and from Falldale's middle-to-end sections (the tonal shift from
comedy to tragedy is deliberate and permanent).

## Multi-Story Focalization Profiles (Hypothesis 037)

The technique profiles differ by paradigm:

**First-person alternating (THLB):** Maximum transparency (both characters'
inner lives fully visible via DT), dual-POV structural dramatic irony as the
primary technique, no FID possible. The reader's experience is one of holding
two complete knowledge-states simultaneously.

**Third-person soft limited romance (TEatS, GIYC):** Symmetric access to both
partners via DT, told narration, and some behavioral proxy. The narrator
maintains a warm distance. Telepathic communication (TEatS) creates a private
channel. FID is absent (TEatS) or subordinate (GIYC). These stories build
investment through accumulation of physical affection and small warm moments.

**Third-person ensemble (NTL):** Multiple focalizers, some with very brief
camera time. Uniform narrator voice across all focalizers. Behavioral proxy
via Pokemon physiology is the dominant emotional language. Dramatic irony
through villain-POV inserts. The reader assembles a composite understanding
from partial views — the ensemble pattern the hypothesis predicts.

**Single-POV war narrative (Falldale, Naive TLTT):** Dialogue carries
interiority. Behavioral proxy via externalized action. DT is sparse to absent.
FID is sparse. The reader is a companion observer, not a co-experiencer.
Dramatic irony comes from the reader's structural knowledge of the setting,
not from dual-POV access.

**One-shot (Wish, Falldale):** The single-session format compresses the
technique: Wish uses the narrator's comedic personality plus heavy DT, Falldale
uses dialogue plus externalized action. Both stories have narrower technique
ranges than the multi-chapter works.

The hypothesis that a single track architecture could serve all paradigms with
variable usage density is supported: the same mechanism vocabulary (M1-M3) and
the same DT/FID/behavioral proxy toolkit describe all 7 texts. The proportions
differ sharply by paradigm but the architecture is universal.

## Fabula-Through-Dialogue Replacement (Hypothesis 040)

The naive TLTT chapters confirm the v0 wall: "Fabula-through-dialogue is the
dominant pattern. Nearly all worldbuilding is delivered through characters
explaining things to AJ." The Fleur Bloom lecture degrading into outline notes
reveals thematic propositions "conceived as arguments to be stated rather than
experiences to be shown."

**The later texts use different delivery mechanisms:**
- **TEatS**: Helping Hand IS the theme (partnership). Telepathy IS intimacy.
  Game mechanics serve as thematic infrastructure, not exposition.
- **NTL**: The economic transformation of Kyukonais is SHOWN step by step
  (clearing forest, building sawmills, starting banks). The reader observes
  prosperity being built rather than being told about it.
- **GIYC**: The modeling trauma is EXPERIENCED through Fluttershy's behavioral
  proxy and DT rather than explained. The "armscye" speech is recontextualized
  as absorbed trauma, not expertise.
- **Falldale**: Still has an exposition dump (Ixodida's geopolitical testimony)
  but ALSO shows the invasion's effects through action and Rocky's behavioral
  responses. The exposition dump is the v0 pattern surviving in a war context
  where characters genuinely need to explain a geopolitical situation.

The four replacement techniques the hypothesis predicts:
1. **Behavioral evidence**: Present in every completed text. Pokemon physiology
   as emotional language, the "pink curtain" motif, Rocky's verbal-refusal arc.
2. **Designed incomplete understanding**: AJ's ignorance in naive TLTT is the
   v0 version (driving exposition). But designed ignorance as a reader-inference
   mechanism does not appear strongly in the later texts — the reader generally
   knows what the characters know.
3. **Revelation architecture**: TEatS's Elliot identity reveal, THLB's Raider
   origin reveal, NTL's progressive feudal-system exposure — these use
   sequenced disclosure to create meaning.
4. **Designed mistakes**: Less present. The strongest instance is THLB's Squash
   betrayal (the reader's prior about harmless Squash is demolished). TEatS's
   Firinia-as-savior prior is revised. But designed reader mistakes as a
   systematic delivery mechanism are not a dominant pattern.

## Demonstration Over Declaration

**Universal pattern: bonds are demonstrated behaviorally, not declared
verbally.** Every story with a bond shows love through accumulated physical
and emotional gesture rather than through verbal confession:

- **THLB**: 20 chapters of training-as-bonding, near-confessions caught and
  suppressed, nuzzles and paw-holding. The "I love you" arrives in the final
  chapter after all the behavioral demonstration has made it redundant.
- **TEatS**: "Extensive physical affection as primary bonding demonstration."
  Nuzzles, cuddling, tail-wrapping, paw-holding in every chapter. The
  confession is one of many physical demonstrations, not the defining moment.
- **NTL**: "Accumulation of small warm interactions rather than single
  high-mechanism moments." Terry teaches Lily to read. Windie gives Helena his
  bed. Helena warms Windie. The kisses are payoffs of accumulated behavioral
  evidence.
- **GIYC**: Rarity charges through the mob to find Fluttershy. Fluttershy
  finishes Rarity's dress. Rarity finds her in a bush. The bond is demonstrated
  through four chapters of mutual rescue before the crush is named.
- **Wish**: The Wish mechanic is simultaneously a battle tactic and an
  involuntary confession — physical demonstration that bypasses verbal
  declaration entirely.

Brian's fiction confirms the instinct: bonds are shown, not told, and the
verbal declaration is the culmination, not the mechanism.

## Asymmetric Interiority Access

In multi-focalizer texts, one character consistently receives deeper narrative
access:

- **THLB**: Hickory holds slightly more total narration; his romantic feelings
  are disclosed from Ch 1 while Maple's develop gradually.
- **Wish**: Jason gets 60% of screen time and more interior time. His detention
  reverie is the story's deepest interiority.
- **TEatS**: Roughly symmetric, but Shane's DT is more frequently strategic
  while Valerie's is more emotionally raw.
- **NTL**: Helena dominates focalization across the full story. Windie becomes
  co-protagonist from Ch 11 but never matches her camera time.
- **GIYC**: "Fluttershy is the primary focalizer... Rarity is the secondary."
  Fluttershy gets deeper interiority — the reader inhabits her attraction while
  Rarity's romantic potential is withheld.

The asymmetry is consistent: one partner gets deeper access, creating tension
about the less-accessed partner's feelings. In THLB and Wish, the
deeper-accessed partner is the one whose feelings are disclosed first. In GIYC,
the deeper-accessed partner is the one with the unconfessed crush. The
asymmetry serves the story's dramatic irony — the reader knows more about one
partner's feelings, creating the question "does the other feel the same?"

- **THLB**: Spruce's promise-breaking (structural setup for the
  protection-vs-agency theme), Cedar's backstory (structural parallel), the

- **TEatS**: Class prejudice setup, the economic contrast between Payr and

- **NTL**: The economic development chapters are extensive WI that serves

  argument crystallizes.
- **GIYC**: The modeling scenes build WI about the fashion system that serves
  structural purposes (establishing the trauma) before reaching the thematic

The WI-terminal ratio appears high in Brian's fiction. Structural setup,
characterization enrichment, and information-plant chains are common.
Theme-terminal chains exist but arrive as culminations of extensive structural
groundwork, not as immediate WI→T jumps.

## Embedded Texts and Dreams

Sparse across the corpus:
- **THLB**: One dream sequence (Hickory's romantic dream, Ch 6) — used as a
  shortcut to state desire rather than as a structural device.
- **TEatS**: Elliot's letter (Ch 21) — the single embedded text in the corpus.
  It serves as a plot pivot: the intelligence that saves the kingdom arrives as
  a written document. The letter enables exposition from a character who cannot
  speak in person.
- No embedded texts, journals, letters, or dream sequences in the other 5 texts.

This is a finding about what Brian does NOT instinctively do. The 112-story
corpus shows embedded texts creating doubled reading positions and access-mode
shifts. Brian's fiction does not use this technique. The one instance (Elliot's
letter) is functional rather than structural — it solves a communication
problem rather than creating a double interpretive layer.

Present. THLB's dual-POV irony is deliberate information architecture — the

Less clearly present. The analyses document reader investment accumulating

Present. THLB's Custap berry promise (childhood → adult fulfillment). TEatS's

Brian designs structural correspondence. The planner should support it.

## Perspective Mode in Ensemble Stories

TEatS and NTL were labeled as omniscient by NotebookLM. The analyses find:

- **TEatS**: "Third-person limited throughout, with soft access to the
  focalizer's thoughts... does not achieve deep third / Free Indirect Discourse
  in any sustained way." Not omniscient — rotated soft limited.
- **NTL**: "Third-person limited, soft... The narration stays close to the
  focalizer's perspective and vocabulary but rarely descends into deep
  interiority." Not omniscient — rotated soft limited with occasional
  multi-character access in group scenes.

Both are rotated limited third, not omniscient. Brian's instinct aligns with
the corpus pattern (5/6 ensemble stories use rotated limited). The "soft"
qualifier matters — the narrator stays close to the focalizer but never fully
dissolves into their consciousness, which can read as omniscient to a surface
analysis. It is not. The perspective discipline in both stories is strong:
knowledge boundaries are respected, POV shifts occur at scene breaks, and
brief villain-POV inserts are deliberate dramatic irony devices rather than
perspective breaches.

## The Adversarial Inner Voice

A distinctive pattern where DT operates as an internal debate between
competing impulses:

- **THLB**: "Yes! Now I can have Maple!" / "No, don't think like that."
  Desire vs. conscience, in both protagonists.
- **Wish**: "I'm totally not being a creep" — self-justification as a form of
  internal debate.
- **TEatS**: Valerie's guilt cycle debates — self-blame vs. determination.
- **GIYC**: Fluttershy's self-recrimination ("I must be a horrible pony")
  as a hostile interlocutor to her own self-worth.
- **NTL**: Windie's inner debates about noble admiration vs. noble rejection.

Absent from Falldale (no DT at all) and naive TLTT (sparse DT, no internal
debates). Present in 5 of 7 texts. This is a distinctive instinctive
technique: the character's marked interiority operates as a split-self
dialogue between desire and conscience, fear and determination, self-worth
and self-destruction. The 112-story corpus identified the adversarial inner
voice as a perception-gap delivery mechanism in Salvation. Brian uses it as
an interiority technique rather than a perception-gap mechanism — the reader
observes the debate rather than inhabiting one side of it.

## The Uniform Narrator Voice

Every multi-focalizer analysis notes the same finding: the narrator's register
does not change when the focalizer changes.

- THLB: "Both narrators use the same register, similar sentence structures."
- TEatS: "It does not shift between focalizers."
- NTL: "The voice stays consistent regardless of the focalizer."
- GIYC: The narrator is "warm, gently comic, affectionate" regardless of
  whether Fluttershy or Rarity holds the camera.

Characters are distinguished by dialogue voice (which is consistently strong
across all texts) and by DT content, but NOT by how the narrator renders
their consciousness. This is the flip side of the FID absence: if the narrator
never dissolves into a character's consciousness, the narrator's own voice
remains constant. It is also the mechanism that prevents M4 — the narrator's
consistent warmth means the reader never fully inhabits one character's biased
self-understanding.

## Framework Fit Assessment

**Where the v4 brief captures Brian's fiction well:**
- M1 (Enacted) is the primary operating mechanism — accurately identified in
  every analysis. Brian's fiction works through clear chains of observation to
  conclusion.
- M2 (Latent) captures Brian's revelation architecture accurately: THLB's
  Squash betrayal, TEatS's Elliot reveal, NTL's feudal-system exposure, naive
  TLTT's Celestia revision, Falldale's Ivy/Ixodida reveal. Brian instinctively
  builds priors and demolishes them.
- M3 (Development) captures the behavioral-proxy arcs: Maple's fear arc, naive
  TLTT's general-title arc, Helena's moral erosion. Brian tracks character
  change through visible behavior.
- The DT/FID diagnostic correctly identifies the M4 ceiling and its cause.

**Where the framework is less precise for Brian's fiction:**
- **Dual-POV structural dramatic irony** is Brian's most distinctive technique
  and falls between the framework's categories. It is not M4 (no FID
  inhabitation), not M2 (no prior demolished), not M3 (no character change
  inferred). It is a structural effect from holding two knowledge states
  simultaneously.
- **Accumulation effects** — the way emotional payoff is built through dozens
  of small moments rather than single high-mechanism instances — operate outside
  the framework's scene-level orientation.
- **Dialogue-as-psychology** — Brian's primary interiority delivery for war
  narratives — sits between Enacted (the reader observes and concludes) and
  Told (the text states outright). Characters state their psychology through

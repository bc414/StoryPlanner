## Hypothesis

Scene-level detail in the v1 archive — specific dialogue, a POV character's in-scene
reaction — is more often scrapped than retained when the fabula content it depends on
later shifts. Unlike fabula-level content, which can survive a shift by being recast as
a Reader Prior Belief Update or Clash note, scene-level detail has no equivalent path
to survive; a shift that invalidates it discards it outright.

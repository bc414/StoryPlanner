## Hypothesis

A TLTT world law that Brian treats as settled enough to act on was reached by negotiating
reasonable canon compliance against materialist historicist analysis, and no third source of
constraint does major work in that negotiation.

## Origin

- date: 2026-09-12
- reasoning: Raised in the design conversation of 2026-08-30, while the epistemic status
  vocabulary for framework hypotheses was being designed, when Brian turned it on his own
  story: "In my story contents, I'm simultaneously making hypotheses and establishing hard
  facts/ontology. It's like studying physics, but I can change the ground truth. However, the
  ground truth is constrained by materialist historicist rigor and most canon." What he meant
  by the state: "'evidenced' meaning acting on it is reasonable is where Newtonian physics
  lies." His assertion, for the world laws: "For layer 1, even this isn't freeform; it has to
  be reasonably canon compliant (e.g. unicorns have horns, pegasi fly and make weather, earth
  ponies are good at farming) and it has to be materialist-historcist compliant, not coming
  from a mythic fable lens (e.g. Severyana is all earth ponies because the weather is too
  harsh for pegasi and unicorns; hippogriffs and seaponies can transform because they evolved
  from the same common ancenstor, not due to an all-powerful Pearl of Transformation;
  changelings have emotion sense because they evolved to metabolize friendship as calories in
  a frozen swamp environment; the world does not have seasons because unicorns move the sun
  and moon on a fixed path, and being able to modify the course of the sun to make variable
  axial tilt would break the worldbuilding ontology into a mythic fable where the sun could be
  dropped on enemies like a nuke)", and "pay special attention to 'reasonable canon
  compliance'. The hippogriff seapony example is one. Canon says there's a mythical Pearl of
  Transformation. I had to reconcile that canon against a materialist historicist reading."
  He named it a hypothesis himself: "That's my hypothesis, that world law ontology is
  'evidenced' against the negotiation between canon compliance and materialist historicist
  analysis. The open part of the hypothesis is whether there are any other major components
  that are part of that negotiation. Must check against the historical lineage/conversations
  corpora." On the second source: "materialist historicist I want a check back on how I
  arrived at that rigor and framework, as it was a multi step process. That process could
  inform potentially other sources of constraint/negotiation", allowing that "it could turn
  out that nothing else stands up besides canon and materialist historicist". Until
  2026-09-12 the claim sat inside a statement that also had the vocabulary replacing the
  planner's note states, and he separated the two: "The current hypothesis statement is not a
  testable assertion about facts. It's mixed with a codebase aspiration." The wording is a
  draft this session wrote from those turns, its exclusive clause turning his open part into a
  prediction; he chose that form over an inclusive one: "Keep just the world law hypothesis."

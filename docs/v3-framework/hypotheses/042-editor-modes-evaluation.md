## Hypothesis

The EditorModes (Expansion, Linking, Gardener, Audit, Scene Design) describe
real cognitive directions that are approximately correct, but the specific mode
designs — what is visible, what is writable, what is excluded in each mode —
may not be optimal and should be evaluated against evidence of actual working
patterns.

## Record

- created | 2026-08-31T20:00: The five modes emerged from conv 36 (planning
  versus writing) and conv 47 (note categorization bootstrapping) as cognitive
  directions: Expansion captures new content, Linking connects subjects to
  scenes, Gardener tends and refines existing content, Audit reviews for
  stability, Scene Design works at the plot-point level. The names and
  directions feel right — Brian uses them as mental labels for what he is doing
  in a session. But the specific UI designs (which tracks are visible, which
  are writable, what is hidden) were designed from first principles during v2's
  build and were never iterated against actual usage. Zero notes were ever
  promoted through Audit mode's note-by-note promotion flow. Scene Design mode
  was never entered for sustained work. The modes may need different visibility
  rules, different write permissions, or different scoping than v2 specified.
  Hypothesis 004 (working-cadence-sweeps) specifically flags Audit mode's
  note-by-note cadence as potentially wrong — that hypothesis is about cadence
  within one mode, while this hypothesis is broader: all five modes' designs
  may need revision based on how Brian actually works versus how the modes
  assumed he would work.

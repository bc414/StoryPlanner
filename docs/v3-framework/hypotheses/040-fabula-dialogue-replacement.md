## Hypothesis

The naive chapters' fabula-through-dialogue delivery is replaced by four
identified techniques: behavioral evidence, designed incomplete understanding,
revelation architecture, and designed mistakes.

## Record

- created | 2026-08-31T20:00: The naive TLTT chapters (June 2025) delivered
  worldbuilding through characters explaining things to each other — fabula
  leaking into the syuzhet. This was the v0 wall that prompted the planner's
  creation: the story needed fabula communicated to the reader, and dialogue
  exposition was the only delivery mechanism Brian had at hand. The four
  replacement techniques emerged from the v2 framework vocabulary and corpus
  analysis. Behavioral evidence: the reader observes actions and infers the
  underlying fabula rather than being told it. Designed incomplete understanding:
  the POV character's cognitive limits generate reader inference — the character
  does not know or misunderstands, and the gap between character knowledge and
  reader knowledge becomes the delivery mechanism. Revelation architecture: the
  deliberate sequencing of when each piece of fabula enters reader awareness,
  so that the order of discovery creates meaning the fabula alone does not carry.
  Designed mistakes: the reader's planned wrong inferences — the reader is led
  to believe something incorrect so that the correction, when it arrives, carries
  force proportional to the confidence of the wrong belief. All four share the
  property that the reader does cognitive work rather than passively receiving
  exposition, and all four are plannable at the scene level (unlike prose
  technique choices).
- evidence | 2026-08-31T23:30 | (WU1.1) [contextual]: The corpus provides
  evidence for all four replacement techniques operating at scale: behavioral
  evidence (M1 behavioral proxy is universal and the dominant technique across
  112 stories), designed incomplete understanding (DT-based knowledge asymmetry
  documented in every category), revelation architecture (M2 prior-demolition
  sequences are the framework's sharpest diagnostic), and designed mistakes
  (self-constructed priors demolished in 5+/11 AU stories and 7/11 explicit/plot
  stories). However, the corpus does not directly test the *replacement*
  prediction — it shows these techniques exist in published stories, not that
  they specifically replace fabula-through-dialogue. That comparison requires
  the naive TLTT chapters (WU1.3/WU1.9).

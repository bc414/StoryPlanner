## Hypothesis

The naive chapters' fabula-through-dialogue delivery is replaced by four
identified techniques: behavioral evidence, designed incomplete understanding,
revelation architecture, and designed mistakes.

## Origin

- date: 2026-08-31
- reasoning: Raised on 2026-08-29 in the corpus-deliberation conversation,
  right after Claude had confirmed that the naive TLTT chapters (June 2025)
  deliver worldbuilding through characters explaining things to each other.
  Brian's own recollection and assertion: "Yes, now I remember that the
  hallmark of chapter 1 and 2 naive, plus Kyukon Universalis/Nine Tales of
  Liberty, is fabula delivered through dialogue. That's what I want to 'fix'
  or evolve when I write TLTT for real. So what is the replacement? The
  fabula exists in the story planner, and the reader infers it? And they are
  free to make mistakes in their inferences too (and that's even designed in
  the narrative architecture)?" The motivation is in that same question:
  replacing dialogue exposition with reader inference, and treating the
  reader's wrong inferences as a designed feature of the narrative
  architecture rather than a flaw to avoid. What raised it to a named
  hypothesis: Claude's answer, in the same exchange, built two further named
  techniques — designed incomplete understanding and revelation architecture
  — onto the inference- and mistake-based replacement Brian had already
  sketched, and the resulting four survived unchanged into the reviewed
  consolidation plan as hypothesis 040.

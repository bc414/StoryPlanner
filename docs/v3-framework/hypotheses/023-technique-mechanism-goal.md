## Hypothesis

A reader-experience-moment — an instance of designed reader operation at a
scene-level intersection — carries three separable properties: a goal (why
the moment exists), a mechanism (the cognitive operation the reader
performs), and a prose technique (how that operation is rendered on the
page). These three vary independently enough that they do not collapse into
one undifferentiated craft choice.

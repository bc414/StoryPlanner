## Hypothesis

The natural working cadence is thorough periodic sweeps rather than incremental
note-by-note hygiene.

## Origin

- date: 2026-08-31
- reasoning: Raised as one of the emergent hypotheses from the 2026-08-31
  design conversation that produced the v3-buildout skill, not captured in
  either the H-series or D-series source documents. Brian's assertion: the
  cadence he actually works in is thorough periodic sweeps across many notes
  at once, not continuous note-by-note tending, and he observed himself
  consistently choosing thorough, batch-oriented options over incremental
  ones during that conversation — matching what he saw as v1's capture
  pattern of periodic expansive sessions rather than steady incremental
  hygiene. What raised it: reconsidering Audit mode, whose design centered on
  locking in `Confirmed` notes one at a time after an expansion phase — a
  cadence he noted was never actually practiced, since v2 has never reached a
  `Confirmed` note under it. The motivation is to settle which cadence the
  planner's baselining process should assume before that process is built
  around one; whether Audit mode's own design got the cadence wrong is left
  to the record, not asserted here.

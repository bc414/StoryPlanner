## Hypothesis

When the session that searched for a pattern also writes the "would differ if false"
clause, non-diagnostic findings pass as supporting evidence at a materially higher rate
than when a fresh-context referee attempts the clause blind; a blind re-check of
pre-revision evidence entries will demote a non-trivial share of them.

  the retroactive referee pass can refute it.

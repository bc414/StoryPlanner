## Hypothesis

A stronger model's advantage over a cheaper one (Fable over Sonnet as the
instance) is concentrated in salience-discretion work and small in
frozen-predicate work under a calibrated codebook; instructional scaffolding
reduces the aggregate gap by moving work from salience cells into
frozen-predicate cells, not by reducing the gap within a cell.

## Hypothesis

Fable's value is primarily in open-ended work when the instructional apparatus
lacks definition (immature skills, absent or thin system prompts), and
diminishes as instructional scaffolding matures — though the competing
possibility that Fable's capability ceiling exceeds other models regardless of
scaffolding maturity remains untested.

- reasoning: Raised in Brian's own notes on decoupling the model/harness/instruction

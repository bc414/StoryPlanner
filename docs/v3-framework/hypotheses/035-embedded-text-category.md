## Hypothesis

Embedded texts and dreams sit outside the standard mechanism categories (M1-M4)
as a distinct structural device that frames reader interpretation differently
from the embedding narrative's own mechanism.

## Origin

- date: 2026-08-31
- reasoning: One of the eight hypotheses drawn from the 2026-08-17 meta-analysis
  of 42 stories, which named dream sequences among the mechanism framework's
  recurring blind spots. Responding to it the next day, Brian named embedded
  texts as an equally live case for TLTT, citing his own manuscript's use of the
  device: the dream sequence linking AJ's arc back to P&K, the AJ-Twilight
  letters across the Agency/Tempest/Crash chapters, the Reni-Minette
  correspondence during the Cartel arc, and the Grover III-Celestia letters.
  The session's working answer, in reply, held that both devices fall outside
  the M1-M4 mechanism levels but should be routed through existing craft-note
  tracks rather than a new mechanism category; the consolidated hypothesis set
  carried forward only the first half of that answer. What is open is whether
  embedding does interpretive work the four mechanisms cannot capture, or is
  fully accounted for as delivery detail on tracks that already exist.

## What you are given

One chapter of a Fimfiction story: the story's slug, the chapter's number and title,
then the chapter's text as the file holds it, italics as *...*. You have nothing else
of the story.

## How to read

The question in view is what prose techniques are used in these stories. A prose
technique is a way the reader is made to interpret the words on the page: the reader
takes the words as something, and a technique is what they are taken as. Read the
whole chapter, then name each distinct way you found, in your own terms. For each,
say as what the reader takes the words, what on the page marks it, and where in the
chapter it occurs, coarsely and in words. What you noticed about the words but cannot
say the reader takes them as goes under unplaced, with its marker.

## What to produce

- techniques: list of line, one per technique: its name | as what the reader takes the words | what marks it, a few words of the page allowed | where in the chapter, in words
- unplaced: list of line, one per thing you noticed that you cannot say the reader takes the words as: what it is | what marks it | where in the chapter, in words; empty if none

## Never

Never report what the reader learns, what is set up or revealed, or the feeling a
passage produces. Never give a position: no line or paragraph numbers, and no
quotation used to say where.

## What you are given

One chapter of a Fimfiction story: the story's slug, the chapter's number and title,
then the chapter's text as the file holds it, italics as *...*. You have nothing else
of the story.

## How to read

The question in view is what prose techniques are used in these stories. A prose
technique is a way the reader is made to interpret the words on the page. Read the
whole chapter, then name each distinct way you found, in your own terms. For each,
say what on the page marks it and where in the chapter it occurs, coarsely and in
words. What you cannot name with confidence goes under unplaced, with its marker.

## What to produce

- techniques: list of line, one per technique: its name | what marks it, a few words of the page allowed | where in the chapter, in words
- unplaced: list of line, one per way of taking the words you could not name: what marks it | where in the chapter, in words; empty if none

## Never

Never report what the reader learns, what is set up or revealed, or the feeling a
passage produces. Never give a position: no line or paragraph numbers, and no
quotation used to say where.

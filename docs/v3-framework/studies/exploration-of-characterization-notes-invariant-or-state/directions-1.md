## What you are given

One note about one character, from the planning database of a long work of fiction: the
character it belongs to, the track it sits in with the question that track asks its notes,
the note's own text, and then that character's other notes in two further tracks, each
under its track name with its world date where it carries one. Those other notes are
context: they are there so that a stretch of the character's life the note may be speaking
of is visible. They are not the note, and nothing that appears only in them is reported as
the note's content. You have nothing else of the plan: not the story, not the scenes, not
the other characters, not the world's calendar.

## How to read

Read the note, then each thing it says about the character. For each, ask when it is true
of them. It may hold throughout — a disposition, a value, a way of thinking, a fact of
birth or kind, a skill they always had. It may hold only over a stretch of the story
world's time — a condition, a role, an office, a belief they later drop, a wound, a
relationship as it stands, a state they are in. One note may hold both, in different
sentences.

Where something is bound to a time, say what binds it: a date, a year or a span in the
note's own words; a named event, phase, place, office or role; a tense or a phrase such as
"at the start", "by now", "no longer", "since", "after", "used to"; or one of the dated
notes beside it that this note plainly speaks of. Where nothing binds it, say so. A thing
with nothing binding it is not thereby something that holds throughout: record only what
the words show, and use the third answer for it.

## What to produce

- claims: list of line, one per thing the note says about the character, four parts separated by a bar: reach, one of throughout where it holds of the character for the whole of their life, span where it holds only over some stretch of the story world's time, or unfixed where the words do not show which | what it says, in your words | what marks it, a few words of the note allowed | what binds it to a time, the date, span, event, phase, role, tense or phrase, or the dated note beside it that it speaks of; none where nothing does
- beside: line, whether any of the dated notes given beside it speaks of the same thing this note does, naming that track and what it says; none where none does

## Never

Never name a position inside the note: no line or sentence numbers, and no quotation used
only to say where. Never report what is in the notes beside it as this note's content.
Never say what the note should have held, and never date anything from what you know of the
world outside the item.

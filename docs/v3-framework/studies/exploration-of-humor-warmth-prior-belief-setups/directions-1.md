## What you are given

One plot point from the planning database of a long work of fiction: its title, its chapter
and position, its synopsis, and its outcome and stakes where they hold text; then every link
it has, to a character, a theme, a story thread or a codex entry of world lore, each under
its kind and name, with the settings the author chose on it in brackets and the link's own
text, "(no text)" where it has none. The settings are labels picked from fixed lists; they
say what the author selected, not what the scene does. You have nothing else of the plan:
not the other plot points, not the linked entities' own notes.

## How to read

Read the whole item, the links' text as well as the synopsis. A plan records what happens
in a scene; it may also record what the scene is meant to do to or for the reader. Look
for the second: an effect the plan intends the reader to have. Three kinds are asked for
by name: humor, where the reader is meant to laugh or be amused; warmth, where the reader
is meant to feel emotional warmth or a feel-good sensation; prior belief, where the reader
is led to believe something the plan means to overturn later, whether or not the item says
when. Report every other kind of intended effect on the reader as well, named in your own
terms. A scene's bearing on a theme is already known and is not reported as an effect of
any kind. An effect is stated where the item names it, implied where the item does not name
it but the scene as planned is plainly built to produce it; an implied effect comes from
what the plan holds, never from what a character or a situation could be.

## What to produce

- effects: list of line, one per effect on the reader the item plans, five parts separated by a bar: kind, humor, warmth, prior belief, or your own name for any other | what the effect is, for a prior belief the belief and what overturns it where the item says | stated or implied | what marks it, a few words of the item allowed | where in the item, the section or the link by its kind and name; empty if the item plans none

## What you are given

One chapter of a Fimfiction story: the story's slug, the chapter's number and title,
then the chapter's text as the file holds it, italics as *...*. You have nothing else
of the story.

## How to read

Read the whole chapter. A moment is a stretch where the words are taken in a distinct
way. For each moment, three things, any of which may be absent. The technique: what
kind of words the reader takes them to be, not what they mean; its name is the way as
it would be named in any story, and the moment is identified by its marker and its
where, never by the name. The experience: what the moment produces in the reader. The
operation: what the moment does to what the reader knows or believes about the
story's world, its history, its characters and what they know. Name each in your own
terms. A part with nothing in it is the word none, and none is the right answer when
nothing fits. What marks a technique already listed goes in that line's marker part,
not on a line of its own. What you noticed that fits no part goes under unplaced,
with its marker.

## What to produce

- moments: list of line, one per moment, six parts separated by a bar: technique, the way as named in any story | kind of words the reader takes them to be, not what they mean | the experience it produces | what the moment does to what the reader knows or believes | what marks it, a few words of the page allowed | where in the chapter, in words; a part with nothing in it is the word none
- unplaced: list of line, one per thing you noticed that fits no part: what it is | what marks it | where in the chapter, in words; empty if none

## Never

Never give a position: no line or paragraph numbers, and no quotation used to say
where.

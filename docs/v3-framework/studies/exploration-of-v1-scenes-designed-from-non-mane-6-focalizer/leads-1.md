## Proposed questions

- Of the 203 plot points whose focalizer is undetermined, how many the working plan now records a POV for,
  and do the two agree where both exist?
- The non-Mane-6 focalizers appear only from chapter 6 onward and thicken toward the end: did the plan acquire
  them as it went, or were the early chapters written with a rule the later ones dropped?
- Where a plot point shows none of the six present, does the working plan hold it as a plot point at all?
- Trimmel, Fleur, Celestia, Henri, Minette and Luna account for 32 of the 44 non-Mane-6 focalizers: do those
  six characters carry subject-level notes that plan them as focalizers?
- Where a plot point carries side passages for several characters and names no focalizer, is the scene one the
  plan means to split, or one whose focalizer was never decided?
